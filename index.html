Your Learning Journey

A structured learning pathway that takes you from foundational knowledge to specialised training, practical projects and professional development.

Choose Your School

01

Choose Your School

Explore the Sodbridge Academy schools and choose a learning pathway that aligns with your interests and career direction.

Available schools: Data School, Software Engineering School, Design School and Cybersecurity School.

Free learning access or an assessed certification pathway.

02

Foundations Programme

Build essential knowledge and practical skills through prerecorded lessons, weekly live sessions, learning materials and guided exercises.

Audit Learner FREE

Access learning materials, prerecorded lessons, weekly live sessions and practice activities without formal certification.

Certified Learner ₦20,000

Access the full learning experience, including formal assignment assessment, feedback, certificate eligibility, certified leaderboard participation and eligibility for additional programme benefits.

Certified learners may qualify for a tuition credit towards Advanced Professional training or a completion refund, subject to the applicable programme requirements.

Readiness Assessment

03

Readiness Assessment

Demonstrate your understanding of the foundational concepts and practical skills required for advanced professional training.

Certified learners participate in the formal assessment process. Progression to Advanced Professional training is subject to meeting the relevant readiness requirements.

Focused professional specialisation, technical development and guided projects.

04

Advanced Professional Programme

Progress into specialised professional training. Work with relevant industry tools, technical workflows, collaborative practices and increasingly complex projects.

Programme pathways: Specialisations vary by school and may run for 8, 12 or 16 weeks.
Training combines instructor-led sessions, independent practice, assignments and practical application.

Practical Capstone

05

Practical Capstone

Apply what you have learned to a practical project that addresses a defined problem or demonstrates a relevant professional capability.

Develop project deliverables, document your approach and present your work for evaluation.

Recognition of successful programme completion.

06

Certification

Learners who successfully meet the programme's completion and assessment requirements receive a Sodbridge Academy certificate.

Professional Development

07

Professional Development

Continue developing your professional profile through portfolio building, CV guidance, interview preparation and access to relevant learning and career opportunities.

Opportunities to apply your skills in practical environments.

08

Industry Experience

Eligible learners and graduates may be considered for internships, supervised practical assignments, collaborative projects and other industry exposure opportunities where available.

These opportunities are subject to availability, selection criteria and applicable programme arrangements. They are not guaranteed.
