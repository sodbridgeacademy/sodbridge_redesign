ABOUT SIEP

Turning Education Into Experience.

The Sodbridge Industry Experience Program (SIEP) is the practical execution arm of Sodbridge Academy. Rather than stopping at classroom learning, SIEP enables selected participants to gain meaningful industry experience by contributing to products, research, analytics, open-source initiatives, and client projects.

Participants work in multidisciplinary teams alongside mentors, applying technical skills to solve real-world challenges while building portfolios that demonstrate measurable impact.

WHY SIEP

Bridging The Gap Between Learning And Industry.

Many graduates complete courses but never gain meaningful experience. SIEP was created to bridge that gap by giving participants opportunities to build products, conduct research, analyse data, collaborate with multidisciplinary teams, and contribute to initiatives that create measurable value.

What You'll Experience

SIEP immerses you in a professional product environment where you build, collaborate, deliver, and prepare for industry—not through simulations, but through meaningful work.

06 Core Experiences

Build Real Products

Contribute to internal products, startup ideas, client solutions, and digital platforms that solve real problems. Your work becomes part of solutions used beyond the classroom.

Collaborate Like Industry Teams

Work in multidisciplinary teams using Agile workflows, Git, design reviews, sprint planning, stand-ups, and collaborative problem-solving across software, data, design, and cybersecurity.

Learn From Industry Mentors

Receive continuous mentorship through technical reviews, pair programming, design critiques, career coaching, and feedback from experienced professionals.

Take Ownership

Take responsibility for assigned features, meet deadlines, present progress, and contribute meaningfully to product goals.

Build a Career Portfolio

Graduate with verified projects, GitHub contributions, case studies, research publications, dashboards, presentations, and documented industry experience.

Grow Into Leadership

Outstanding contributors may become SIEP Fellows, lead project teams, mentor future cohorts, receive performance-based recognition, and be considered for strategic initiatives and future opportunities.
