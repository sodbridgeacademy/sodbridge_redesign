Frequently Asked Questions

Is Foundations really free? expand_more
Yes. Our weekly public Foundations sessions are available free through the Sodbridge Academy YouTube channel. An optional ₦5,000 structured-learning pathway is available for learners who want additional support, structure and a Foundations certificate.
What does the ₦5,000 cover? expand_more
The ₦5,000 is an optional structured-learning fee. It provides learners with a more organized learning experience, additional guidance and access to the structured Foundations pathway. The full ₦5,000 is credited toward tuition if you later enrol in an eligible Advanced Programme.
Can I apply directly for Advanced? expand_more
Yes. Foundations is recommended for beginners, but learners with relevant prior knowledge, education or experience may apply directly. Direct applicants may be required to complete a readiness assessment before admission.
Do I receive a certificate from Foundations? expand_more
Foundations certificates are available to learners enrolled in the optional structured-learning pathway who successfully complete the programme requirements. Learners who participate through the free public YouTube sessions do not automatically receive a certificate.
What happens after Foundations? expand_more
Learners who successfully complete the Foundations pathway can progress toward an Advanced Programme. The Advanced Programme is more intensive and focuses on deeper technical skills, practical projects and professional workflows.
Who can join Sodbridge Academy? expand_more
Our programmes are open to students, recent graduates, NYSC corps members, career changers, professionals and anyone committed to developing practical technology skills.
