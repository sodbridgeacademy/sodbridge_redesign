Frequently Asked Questions

Is Foundations really free? expand_more
Yes. The Audit pathway is free and includes access to the shared Foundations learning experience, including recorded lessons, shared live classes, learning activities and community events. Learners who want formal assessment and certification can choose Certified Foundations for ₦10,000.
What does the ₦10,000 Certified Foundations fee cover? expand_more
The fee covers access to the formal assessment and certification pathway, including track-specific assessment and recorded results with rubric-based feedback. A Sodbridge Academy Foundations Certificate is awarded only to learners who meet the published requirements. The full ₦10,000 is credited toward eligible Professional Programme tuition. Eligible programmes and the credit validity period must be stated in the published terms before payment.
Can I apply directly for a Professional Programme? expand_more
Yes. Foundations is recommended for beginners, but learners with relevant prior knowledge, education or experience may apply directly. Direct applicants may be required to complete a readiness assessment before admission.
Do I receive a certificate from Foundations? expand_more
Audit learners do not automatically receive a Foundations certificate. Certified Foundations learners must complete the required learning activities and meet the published assessment criteria before a certificate is awarded.
What happens after Foundations? expand_more
Eligible learners can apply for a Professional Programme. Certified Foundations learners may use their ₦10,000 tuition credit toward an eligible programme under the published terms. The top 10% of eligible Certified Foundations learners can compete for a 50% merit discount. Direct applicants with relevant prior skills or experience may also apply through a readiness assessment.
Who can join Sodbridge Academy? expand_more
Our programmes are open to students, recent graduates, NYSC corps members, career changers, professionals and anyone committed to developing practical technology skills.
How is the 50% Professional Programme discount awarded? expand_more
The discount is a merit award for the top 10% of eligible Certified Foundations learners. Ranking is based on the published assessment criteria, not payment or attendance alone. Eligibility, ranking rules and how the tuition credit combines with the discount will be published before the cohort begins.
Does Certified Foundations guarantee an internship? expand_more
No. Certified learners may apply for limited supervised internship placements. Placements depend on availability, selection criteria, learner readiness and supervision capacity.
