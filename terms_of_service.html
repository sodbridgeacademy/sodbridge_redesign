2. Program Eligibility

To be eligible for the Sodbridge Industry Experience Program, applicants must meet the following criteria:

check_circle Currently enrolled in or a recent graduate of a tertiary institution.
check_circle Demonstrated proficiency in foundational technical or creative skills.

3. Application Fees

To maintain the high standard of our selection process, a non-refundable application processing fee of ₦5,000 (Five Thousand Naira) is required from all applicants.

This fee covers the administrative costs of technical assessments, portfolio reviews, and interview scheduling. Payment of the application fee does not guarantee admission into the program.

4. Payments & Refund Policy

Program Fees

Once admitted, students must adhere to the agreed payment schedule. Failure to complete payments by the specified deadlines may result in temporary suspension of access to program resources.

Refund Policy

Refund requests for program tuition must be submitted in writing within 7 days of the program start date. A processing fee will be deducted. After the 7-day window, no refunds will be issued under any circumstances.

groups Student Responsibilities

Students are expected to attend all synchronous sessions, contribute actively to group projects, and maintain a professional level of communication with mentors and peers.

We maintain a zero-tolerance policy for plagiarism. All submitted work must be original or appropriately credited. Violations will result in immediate expulsion without refund.
