Foundations Programme

Choose How You Want to Learn

Everyone can access the Foundations learning experience. Choose free audit participation or become a Certified Learner to access formal assessment, certification and additional programme benefits.

Option 1 FREE

Audit Learner

Learn at no cost and participate in the Foundations learning experience without enrolling in the formal certification pathway.

  • check_circle Access to prerecorded learning videos
  • check_circle Participation in weekly live sessions
  • check_circle Access to learning materials and resources
  • check_circle Participation in practice activities and design challenges
  • check_circle Opportunity to explore design specialisations

Please note

Audit Learners are not eligible for formal assessment, the Foundations Certificate, certified leaderboard participation or benefits reserved for Certified Learners.

Option 2 ₦20,000

Certified Learner

Take the structured learning and assessment pathway, demonstrate your skills and become eligible for certification and additional programme benefits.

  • check_circle Everything included in Audit participation
  • check_circle Formal assignments and assessments
  • check_circle Structured instructor feedback
  • check_circle Eligibility for the Foundations Certificate upon successful completion
  • check_circle Participation in the certified learner leaderboard
  • check_circle Eligibility for applicable programme progression benefits

Your ₦20,000 doesn't have to end with Foundations

If you progress to an eligible Advanced Professional Programme, the full ₦20,000 is credited towards your tuition. Alternatively, eligible learners who complete Foundations without progressing may qualify for a 70% refund (₦14,000), subject to the programme's completion and performance requirements. These are alternative benefits and cannot be combined.
